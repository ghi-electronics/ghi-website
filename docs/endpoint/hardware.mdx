---
title: "Endpoint Hardware"
---

Endpoint runs on a family of hardware designed for different form factors
and production needs. A System on Module and a production dev board are
planned. The only board available today is the evaluation board,
**Endpoint Domino24**.

- [Endpoint Domino24](domino.mdx): low-cost evaluation / prototyping single-board computer. Display FPC connector, 2× mikroBUS headers, USB host, 40-pin expansion header, microSD card slot. Our recommended starting point.

:::note[Evaluation hardware]
Domino24 is the evaluation board we built for Endpoint. We have not released
SoMs or dev boards yet, because we are still shaping what the commercial
product should be. Tell us what you need on the
[forum](https://forums.ghielectronics.com/): what form factor, which
peripherals, and whether wireless is a requirement.
:::
