---
title: "Endpoint Hardware"
---

Endpoint runs on a family of hardware designed for different form factors
and production needs. A System on Module and a production dev board are
planned. Today you can evaluate Endpoint on these boards:

- [Endpoint Domino24](endpoint-domino24.mdx): our low-cost evaluation / prototyping single-board computer. Display FPC connector, 2× mikroBUS headers, USB host, 40-pin expansion header, microSD card slot. Our recommended starting point.
- [Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W](raspberry-pi-zero.mdx): a third-party evaluation option. Same extension, libraries, and APIs, with fewer hardware features.

:::note[Evaluation hardware]
These boards are for evaluation. We have not released SoMs or dev boards
yet, because we are still shaping what the commercial product should be.
Tell us what you need on the
[forum](https://forums.ghielectronics.com/c/endpoint/34): what form factor, which
peripherals, and whether wireless is a requirement.
:::
