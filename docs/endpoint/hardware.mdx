title: "Endpoint Hardware"
---

Endpoint runs on a family of hardware designed for different form factors
and production needs. More options — a System on Module, a dev board,
and a Nuvoton-based variant — are planned; the only board available
today is the evaluation board, **Endpoint Domino**.

- [Endpoint Domino](domino.mdx) — low-cost evaluation / prototyping single-board computer. Display FPC connector, 2× mikroBUS headers, USB host, 40-pin expansion header, microSD card slot. Our recommended starting point.

:::note[Evaluation hardware]
Domino is the evaluation board we built for Endpoint. We have not released
SoMs or dev boards yet, because we are still shaping what the commercial
product should be. Tell us what you need on the
[forum](https://forums.ghielectronics.com/): what form factor, which
peripherals, and whether wireless is a requirement.
:::
