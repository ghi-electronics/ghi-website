## A new platform, and your first taste of it

Endpoint is new, and we are still shaping what the commercial product should be. We have not released SoMs or dev boards yet, because we would rather get that right than ship early. Tell us on the [forum](https://forums.ghielectronics.com/c/endpoint/34) what form factor you need, which peripherals matter, and whether wireless is a requirement or a nice to have. Those answers shape what we build next.

**Endpoint Domino24** is how you try the platform today. We built it for evaluation and excitement, and the board says so: "For Evaluation and Excitement Only." It's your first taste of what Endpoint can do and a look at what's to come.
